REDONGRA Committees

Committee for the REDONGRA Collaboration Meeting Program

The main goal of the program is to support, both concerning coordination and financial support (co-funding is expected), the organization of meetings/workshops that are crucial for the participation of the REDONGRA groups in the different GW collaborations. We foresee future working group meetings of the different detector collaborations, as well as meetings on specific topics of current interest to the network, such as workshops on Machine Learning Methods or on Source modeling and data analysis for future detectors.

To apply use the form HERE including the following information:

  • Title of the meeting/activity
  • Description (5-10 lines)
  • (Tentative) dates
  • Type of support expected from REDONGRA

Committee for the REDONGRA Exchange Program

This program, mainly oriented toward young researchers, will support short visits (maximum 30 days) of members of the REDONGRA groups to other REDONGRA nodes. There will be two openings for applying for support for such visits (some level of co-funding is expected). The selection will be based on scientific merit. The interested researchers can apply following the information HERE.

Committee for the organization of the IGWM + ISGWP Events

Organization of the yearly Iberian Gravitational Wave Meeting (IGWM) and of a yearly International School on Gravitational Wave Physics (ISGWP). The two activities will be scheduled within the same time frame to optimize resources and ensure the attendance of young researchers, as well as attracting leading figures in the field.

The ISGWP will consist in a series of lectures, mainly addressed to PhD students and young postdocs, and hands-on sessions covering all relevant aspects of GW science (instrumentation, simulations, data analysis algorithms, theory), to be delivered by internationally recognized experts.

DEI-Outreach Committee

Many of the groups involved in REDONGRA carry out successful public outreach programs, targeting both their local communities and increasing their national visibility through national media. REDONGRA will coordinate public announcements among groups participating in large detector collaborations, to highlight collaboration on the national level and the involvement of Spanish groups in breakthrough discoveries and in preparing next generation observatories. REDONGRA will also produce high quality printed and electronic outreach media.

Roadmap Committee

An important outcome of the REDONGRA network will be a roadmap document that will identify the main activities of the Spanish GW community and a concrete path towards maximizing its impact on current and future gravitational wave detectors.

Members:
  • All REDONGRA members + invited experts in areas of interest
